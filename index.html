What is happening at the University of Layyah

  1. 12 Oct 2026

    Fall admissions are open

    Applications are open for BS programs. Submit your form and documents before the deadline.

    Collect the form from the admissions office or download it from the university website. Bring attested copies of your certificates, four photographs and your CNIC or B-Form. Merit lists will be displayed on the notice board.

  2. 5 Oct 2026

    Annual Science Fair

    Students from every department will present projects in robotics, agriculture and software.

    The fair runs for two days in the main hall. Visitors are welcome, and the best projects will receive prizes from the vice chancellor.

  3. 28 Sep 2026

    Cricket team wins inter-university match

    The university team won a close final and moves on to the regional round next month.

    The team chased down the target with two overs to spare. Practice sessions continue every evening at the university ground.

  4. 20 Sep 2026

    New computer lab inaugurated

    The Computer Science department now has a lab with 40 workstations for programming and web classes.

    The lab is open on weekdays and has high-speed internet. Students can book extra hours for project work through their department.

  5. 10 Sep 2026

    Tree plantation drive on campus

    Faculty and students planted over 200 saplings, including date palms, across the campus.

    Each department adopted a section of the campus and will look after its trees through the year.

  6. 1 Sep 2026

    Midterm exam schedule released

    The date sheet for all departments is available on the notice boards.

    Check your department notice board for room numbers and timings. Students must bring their university card to every paper.
